Questions

Straight answers before you call

What is the Made in America Loan Guarantee?

It's an enhancement to an existing SBA 7(a) loan program. Since May 1, 2026, SBA guarantees 90% of eligible loans to manufacturers. A participating lender still makes and approves the loan, and the guarantee covers part of the lender's loss if the loan isn't repaid.

Is it a grant, or a new loan program?

Neither. It's a loan from a bank, repaid on normal terms. What changed is how much of that loan SBA guarantees to the lender.

Does my business qualify?

The guarantee is for manufacturers whose primary NAICS code is 31–33, and the business also has to meet SBA's usual size and eligibility rules. We'll tell you on the first call whether it looks like a fit. The lender makes the final call.

What can I use the money for?

Equipment, buying or building space, working capital and expansion: the things it takes to add capacity.

What does it cost?

Nothing up front. If your loan closes, our packaging fee (typically about $2,500) is disclosed on SBA Form 159, and the lender pays our referral fee.

Can you guarantee my loan will be approved?

No. The lender decides. Our job is to put the strongest, most complete package in front of lenders who finance manufacturers.

How long does it take?

It depends on the lender and on how ready your books are. We won't promise a date, but we'll give you an honest read on the first call.

Do you work for the bank or for SBA?

Neither. Cypress Grove Partners is an independent SBA agent and is not affiliated with SBA or any lender. We work with lenders active in manufacturing.

What do I need for the first call?

Nothing. If it's a fit, we'll send a short document list and a secure link to upload them.

Where do you work?

Hillsborough, Pinellas, Pasco, Polk and Manatee counties. We're based in Odessa and come to your shop.
