Where I come from

Shop floor first, then the lender's desk

I studied industrial engineering at the University of Miami and started in consulting at an engineering firm, learning how manufacturing is measured and improved. After my MBA I worked at Fireblocks and Palantir, in financial infrastructure and AI. Now I bring that home to the shops building here.

What that means in practice: I can read a capacity plan and a debt schedule in the same afternoon. Most of a loan application is proving a shop can carry the payment — and most of Prime Readiness is proving a shop can be trusted with the work.
