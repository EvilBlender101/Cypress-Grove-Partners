Privacy

Cypress Grove Partners LLC ("we") runs this website. This page explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect. This site has no forms, no advertising and no tracking cookies. We receive information only when you choose to contact us: by email, by phone, or by booking a call through Google Calendar, which shares your name, email and the details you enter with us.

Loan documents. If we work together, we send you a secure link to upload documents. Files are encrypted in your browser before they leave your computer. We use your documents only to prepare your loan package and share them only with lenders you agree to, and with SBA as the loan process requires.

What we don't do. We don't sell or rent your information, and we never hold client funds or access your accounts.

Questions or deletion requests. Email jack@cypressgrove.partners.

Terms of use

The information on this site is general and is not legal, tax or financial advice. Program terms are set by SBA and by lenders, and they can change.

Cypress Grove Partners is not affiliated with the U.S. Small Business Administration or any lender. Lenders make all credit decisions, and approval is not guaranteed. Any fee we charge is disclosed in writing before you engage us and on SBA Form 159.
